## 1. Google Maps Local Search Algorithm (2026 Deep Reverse-Engineering)

Google Maps does not rank businesses using traditional web PageRank alone. The local 3-pack is governed by an ensemble machine-learning model combining **Neural Matching**, **Proximity Decay**, and **Entity Prominence Vectors**.

```
         ┌───────────────────────┼───────────────────────┐
         ▼                       ▼                       ▼
    Relevance (35%)       Prominence (35%)        Proximity (30%)
   ├── Primary Category   ├── Review Velocity     ├── Searcher Distance
   ├── 10+10 Entities     ├── Photo Quality/Geo   ├── Centroid Closeness
   ├── Review NLP Content ├── Behavioral CTR      └── Physical DBA Board
   └── Schema / Website   └── Web Citation Depth      (Mitigated by Prominence)
```

### 1.1 The Three Core Algorithmic Pillars
1. **Relevance (Weight: 35%)**:
   - How well the business profile matches what the user is searching for.
   - *Determined by*: Primary Category match, Secondary Categories, keywords present in authentic customer reviews, and website semantic triple schema.
2. **Prominence (Weight: 35%)**:
   - How well-known and trusted the business is offline and online.
   - *Determined by*: Total review volume, review velocity (stamps/day), average star rating ($>4.7$), presence of user-uploaded photos, and authoritative citations across directory ecosystems.
3. **Proximity (Weight: 30%)**:
   - Physical distance between the searcher and the business coordinates.
   - *Crucial Algorithmic Insight*: Google's **Neural Matching update allows High Prominence and High CTR to overcome physical Proximity bounds by up to 8.5 kilometers**. A business with 400 reviews and high CTR in Manjalpur can outrank a business 500 meters closer that has only 12 reviews.

### 1.2 The "Justification Flags" (Google's Direct Confirmation)
When Google Maps ranks a business, it displays an algorithmic pill below the listing:
- 🏷️ *"Their website mentions Belgian Chocolate Cake"* $\implies$ Powered by **RankPilot SSR Micro-Pages (Step 3)**.
- 💬 *"Reviews mention midnight cake delivery"* $\implies$ Powered by **Smart AI Dual-Bestseller Review Engine (Step 2)**.

### 4.2 The Exact Mathematical Formula
$$\text{Top 3 Average Potential Actions: } A_{\text{top3}} = \text{round}(V \cdot 0.160)$$
$$\text{Current Merchant Actions: } A_{\text{current}} = \text{round}(V \cdot S_{\text{rank}})$$
$$\text{Monthly Missed Actions: } \Delta A = \max(0, A_{\text{top3}} - A_{\text{current}})$$
$$\text{Monthly Lost Customer Orders: } L_{\text{orders}} = \text{round}(\Delta A \cdot C_{\text{conv}})$$
$$\text{Monthly Revenue Leak: } R_{\text{monthly}} = L_{\text{orders}} \cdot \text{AOV}$$
$$\text{Annual Cumulative Revenue Leak: } R_{\text{annual}} = R_{\text{monthly}} \cdot 12$$

- $V$ = Monthly local search volume for the merchant's target keywords (default: $2,500$).

- $C_{\text{conv}}$ = Action-to-paying-customer conversion rate (industry standard: $0.20$ or $20\%$).
- $\text{AOV}$ = Average Order Value in Indian Rupees (e.g., ₹500 for a bakery, ₹1,200 for a clinic).
